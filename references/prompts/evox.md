## Layout

Use a cinematic 2.35:1 ultra-wide horizontal composition.

Place the EvoX logo + wordmark on the left side or slightly left of center.

Place the subtitle directly underneath:

**Agent还能这么玩**

The logo/title and subtitle should NOT be oversized.

Keep generous negative space around the typography.

The title block should occupy roughly 25–35% of the visual width rather than dominating the entire cover.

The background itself should remain an important visual element.

## Background

Create a sophisticated dark honeycomb environment inspired by the uploaded EvoX reference image.

Do NOT simply create a flat repeating hexagonal pattern.

Instead, build a visually rich honeycomb system:

- deep three-dimensional hexagonal structures
- some cells illuminated, others disappearing into darkness
- different cells subtly connected through light
- irregular activation across the honeycomb
- subtle depth and perspective
- layered metallic or glass-like materials
- faint energy moving between several cells
- a sense of an intelligent system gradually activating and evolving

The honeycomb can transition from orderly structures into more complex interconnected patterns, subtly suggesting **evolution and autonomous Agent collaboration**.

The visual should feel mysterious, sophisticated and energetic.

## Color direction

Main background:
- near black
- graphite
- charcoal
- dark metallic gray

Accent lighting:
- vivid amber orange
- warm gold
- restrained electric violet / purple
- very subtle cool highlights only where necessary

Use small areas of intense color against a predominantly dark environment.

The colors should feel luminous and premium rather than neon-heavy.

Avoid making blue the dominant color.
