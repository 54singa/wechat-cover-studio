## Background / Main visual

Create a sophisticated **deep-ocean blue environment**.

The background should NOT look like a literal underwater wildlife photograph.

Instead, create an abstract cinematic interpretation of the deep ocean.

Use:

- deep navy blue
- midnight blue
- indigo
- near-black shadows
- saturated electric blue highlights
- very restrained violet highlights

The image should transition naturally from darker negative space on the left into a richer illuminated environment on the right.

### Whale

Place a large whale on the right side of the composition.

The whale should feel:

- elegant
- powerful
- mysterious
- intelligent
- monumental but restrained

Show the whale from a dynamic three-quarter or side angle, swimming diagonally through the composition.

Do not make it cute or cartoonish.

Do not create a conventional nature-documentary whale.

Instead, integrate subtle abstract technological details:

- thin luminous blue trajectories following the whale's movement
- tiny particles suspended in the surrounding darkness
- elegant flowing light paths
- subtle fragmented light patterns appearing around parts of its silhouette
- faint streams suggesting information or Agent execution paths

These technological details should remain abstract and artistic.

The whale should appear to be **emerging from or moving through an intelligent digital ocean**, rather than swimming through an obvious computer interface.
